# Future ideas — not committed

These are **ideas only**: no story files, acceptance criteria, delivery dates or implicit implementation authorization.

- **Language support:** add C, C++, then Free Pascal after secure compiler/runtime limits and fairness constraints are validated independently.
- **Scoring rules:** optionally upload separate validated test weights/scoring files; initial judge reports only pass/fail and measurements.
- **Student practice:** individual submission mode with feedback, still no public user accounts until access controls are designed.
- **Custom checkers:** checker programs or flexible whitespace/tolerance policies only after checker sandboxing is designed.
- **Private/persistent data:** optional accounts, task archives and permission separation; not required by the original single-operator use case.
- **Similarity/plagiarism review:** opt-in heuristic similarity reports reviewed by a human, never automatic accusation or disqualification.
- **Expanded analytics:** aggregate timing comparisons with documented language/hardware limitations.

No GitHub/network integrations beyond explicitly configured Ollama/OpenRouter are planned.
